About this data — read before presenting

  • Coordinates were corrected. In poi__2.db the latitude column holds longitude and vice versa. Every point was swapped back; corrected latitudes are 34.043–34.093 and longitudes −118.337 to −118.248 (central LA, wider than downtown alone).
  • 65 of 484 places have a name. The other 419, including all 34 playgrounds and 312 of 314 swimming pools, show a fallback name such as “Swimming Pool #id”. Do not drop them: dropping unnamed rows would delete most of the dataset.
  • Scores collapse to 9 distinct vectors. Places are scored by type (activities averaged, then scaled by age band), so park and garden share a vector, as do sports centre and fitness centre. Places of the same type differ only by travel time. Say this in the report; it is the dataset's main limitation.
  • Two types had no activities. Dog parks use “free play” and outdoor seating uses “picknick” as documented fallbacks (15 places).
  • Dwell time and cost are assumptions by type (for example playground 45 min, swimming pool 60 min; parks free, others unknown). Opening hours in the source are OSM tags, not hours, and are ignored.
  • Score formula: 0.70 × cosine(profile, place) + 0.30 × age fit − travel penalty (first 15 min free, then 0.01/min, capped 0.20). Age fit is 1.0 for this dataset because age is carried by the age-scaled vectors; changing the age band changes the vectors.
