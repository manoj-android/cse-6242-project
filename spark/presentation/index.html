Data Ingestion Pipeline

SPARK processes public geospatial data from OpenStreetMap and pediatric milestone profiles to generate outing recommendations in Downtown Los Angeles:

Step 1
Southern California OSM Extract Ingested and stored locally in SQLite (`spark/data/poi.db`).
Step 2
Entity Validation & Coordinate Cleaning Canonical WGS84 coordinates verified and indexed.
Step 3
Pediatric Milestone Schema (484 Venues) Relational tables mapping venues to activities and 8 clinical skill dimensions.
Step 4
Interactive Recommendation & Routing Simulated Annealing optimizer and 2-Opt route sequencing.
