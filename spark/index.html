📦 CSE 6242 Big Data Ingestion Funnel

SPARK processes large-scale public geospatial archives from OpenStreetMap and the USGS National Land Cover Database (NLCD) to enable sub-15ms interactive visual decision support in Downtown Los Angeles:

Step 1
Southern California OSM PBF Extract (~12 GB raw, >30 Million nodes) Full regional topological nodes/ways ingested via Python SQLite3 spatial scripts.
Step 2
USGS NLCD 30m Tree Canopy Raster (1.2 GB) Zonal statistics compute shade and thermal comfort percentage for public spaces.
Step 3
Entity Resolution & Deduplication (24,180 → 5,120 Candidates) Spatial proximity gating (≤50m) and Levenshtein string edit distance pruning.
Step 4
Relational Pediatric Taxonomy (poi.db — 484 Venues) 4-table schema linking OSM tags to 11 activity archetypes and 6 age brackets.
Step 5
Active Downtown Los Angeles Corridor (48 Validated Stops) Sub-15ms multi-objective greedy routing, D3 Radar/Stacked Bar charts, and timeline.
