# 2026 Fall CSE6242 / CX4242 Group Project Requirements

## 1. Core Project Components
* **Dataset:** Must be large, real, and public.
    * *Prohibited:* Synthetic (AI-generated) or private/proprietary datasets.
    * *Discouraged:* APIs/web scraping (due to speed/volume limits).
* **Computation:** Must involve non-trivial analysis or algorithms (basic stats like averages or min/max are not enough).
* **User Interface:** Must have an interactive, visual UI that directly interacts with the underlying algorithms.

## 2. Logistics & Guidelines
* **Team Size:** 4 to 6 members. Solo projects are strictly not allowed.
* **Topics:** Open choice, but "double-dipping" (using work from other courses or research) is strictly prohibited.
* **Formatting Penalties:** A strict **5% grade deduction** applies to *every* file with an incorrect name or format.

## 3. Key Deliverables
### Phase 1: Proposal
* **Report:** Max 2 pages (excluding references). Must answer the 9 "Heilmeier questions", include a Gantt chart, and a literature survey (at least 3 papers per member).
* **Presentation:** 2-minute strict limit (in-class for campus, unlisted YouTube video for OMS). 5% penalty for overrunning.

### Phase 2: Progress Checkpoint
* **Report:** Max 4 pages. Details proposed methods, experiment/evaluation plans, and an updated activity schedule.

### Phase 3: Final Submission
* **Report & Code:** Max 6-page report (5 for undergrad-only teams). Requires packaged software with a README, documentation, and optional demo video.
* **Poster & Presentation:** 30x40 inch team poster. *Each* member must individually record a 3-minute unlisted YouTube video.
* **Peer Grading:** Mandatory peer feedback at each stage and presentation grading. Failure to grade peers lowers your own presentation score.
